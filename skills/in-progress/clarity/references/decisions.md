# Decisions

Use when the user is circling options, stuck in analysis paralysis, or framing a choice as an all-or-nothing dilemma.

## Moves

- **Break false binaries.** When the choice is framed as *A vs. B*, test whether a hybrid, sequence (*A now, B later*), or third option (*neither / change the frame*) exists.
- **Name the status-quo baseline.** Doing nothing is an active choice with its own cost and trajectory. Make the cost of staying put explicit alongside the options.
- **Test reversibility.** Distinguish one-way doors (costly or permanent) from two-way doors (cheap to reverse). Treat two-way doors as experiments rather than permanent commitments.
- **Find the kill criterion.** Ask what single fact, number, or observation would cleanly rule an option in or out. Point **Next concrete move** at gathering that single signal.
