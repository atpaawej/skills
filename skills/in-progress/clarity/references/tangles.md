# Tangles

Use when everything feels urgent, too many threads compete at once, or emotion and fact are fused together.

## Moves

- **Separate observation from story.** Rewrite *"everything is falling apart because X hates the plan"* as two lines: what visibly happened (observation) and what is feared or inferred (story).
- **Find the single bottleneck.** List the active threads in one line each, then ask which single thread, if resolved or dropped, unblocks or shrinks the rest. Treat that thread as **the crux**.
- **Audit fake urgency.** Check each thread for a real external deadline vs. self-imposed pressure or discomfort avoidance. Move anything without a real near-term consequence to **noise** (parked).
- **Shrink the horizon.** When the whole situation feels unsolvable, isolate the next 24–72 hours or the smallest subsystem where the user still has direct agency.
